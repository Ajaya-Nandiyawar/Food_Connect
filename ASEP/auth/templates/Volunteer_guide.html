Volunteer Rules and Regulations

Eligibility Requirements

  • Must be at least 18 years of age with valid ID
  • Reliable transportation required
  • Must complete food safety training (provided)
  • Background check required (free of charge)

Volunteer Responsibilities

  • Arrive promptly for scheduled pickups and deliveries
  • Handle food safely according to guidelines
  • Verify food quantities during pickup and delivery
  • Commit to minimum 2 hours weekly for 3 months

Food Safety Guidelines

  • Inspect food quality before accepting from restaurants
  • Use thermal bags to maintain proper food temperatures
  • Complete monthly food safety refresher courses
  • Wear FOODCONNECT ID during volunteer activities
