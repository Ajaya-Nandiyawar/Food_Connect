Food Connect

Food Connect is a platform dedicated to reducing food waste and fighting hunger by creating a bridge between restaurants with surplus fresh food and NGOs that distribute it to people in need. Every day, large amounts of perfectly good food go to waste, while many individuals and families struggle with food insecurity. Our mission is to ensure that no meal is wasted when it can be used to feed someone in need. By streamlining the donation process, we make it easy for restaurants to contribute their excess food while helping NGOs access fresh, nutritious meals for the communities they serve.

Our Vision

At Food Connect, our mission is simple: to unite food lovers, creators, and enthusiasts in an inclusive platform that inspires collaboration, discovery, and the joy of cooking and eating.

Our Mission

We envision a world where food is shared, not thrown away. Every day, millions of tons of perfectly edible food end up in landfills, contributing to environmental degradation. At the same time, millions of people worldwide go hungry.
